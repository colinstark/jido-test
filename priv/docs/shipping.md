# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the EU. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs €9.90.

## International orders
We ship to the UK, Switzerland and Norway. Customs fees are paid by the recipient. We do not currently ship to the US.

## Damaged on arrival
If a widget arrives damaged, open a ticket within 14 days with your order number (ORD-xxxxx) and a photo. We send a replacement free of charge.
