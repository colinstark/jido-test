# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return any unused widget within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened widgets can be returned within 30 days minus a 15% restocking fee.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards and engraved widgets cannot be returned.
