# Widget Pro

## Pairing with the companion app
Hold the power button for 6 seconds until the LED blinks blue, then choose "Add device" in the app. Bluetooth must be enabled and the widget within 2 meters.

## Battery
The battery lasts about 40 hours. A full charge over USB-C takes 90 minutes. The LED turns red below 10%.

## Factory reset
Hold power and volume-down together for 15 seconds. This erases all pairings and settings.

## Warranty
Widget Pro has a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. Water damage is not covered.
